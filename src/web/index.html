How this works & your privacy

Consent-first. The synthetic household sensor only produces observations while capture is enabled and consent is granted. Withdraw either and the gate blocks all delivery — nothing reaches the event pipeline.

Local & private. Everything runs on this machine. No cloud, no network calls, no raw audio/video. Only alert metadata (id, severity, rule, message, timestamp) is stored, in a local SQLite database.

Real backend. Observations flow through the actual C++ event pipeline: validation → time-window correlation → alert dedup/cooldown → durable persistence. Every number and message below comes from that running code, not from this page.

Reading the result

Detected / not detected / inconclusive are the detector's own verdicts on the synthetic evidence — an inconclusive result is never reported as a detection.

Fusion combines detector outputs; when evidence is ambiguous or insufficient it reports inconclusive or unavailable rather than inventing confidence.

Statistical anomaly. A second stage learns the household's recent activity baseline and flags deviations (high/low/missing). A deviation is a statistical finding, not a fall, emergency or danger claim; by default it is advisory and does not vote on the alert.

Alerts are detection-driven. An alert is raised only when the fused detector evidence actually qualifies (the detection rule agrees, above a confidence floor). Raw observations on their own never raise an alert — they are the detector's input. This demo does not claim fall detection, medical emergencies, or real recognition.

Animal activity & environmental correlation. Where this build can measure household activity it reports a bounded activity change, an audio-energy deviation, or — at most — a labelled hypothesis of possible animal activity. An environmental reading that moved at the same time is reported as a correlation, and correlation is not causation. No animal species is identified, and this system never predicts a disaster: it produces no earthquake, flood or storm forecast from animal behaviour, and it identifies no person, no fall and no speech content.
